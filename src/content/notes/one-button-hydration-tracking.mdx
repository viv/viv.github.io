## The Problem

I like to track my water intake throughout the day. I want to be able to spot patterns and correlations between water intake and general health indicators.

I already had a [Home Assistant](https://www.home-assistant.io/) automation hooked up to an [IKEA Bilresa](https://www.ikea.com/gb/en/p/bilresa-remote-control-smart-white-80569089/) dual smart button (these buttons are brilliant, cheap, solidly made, and support Matter over Thread). Press button one, log a pint of water to [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/). Press button two, log a cup of tea. Simple and it worked well enough, but calendar entries aren't great for visualising trends over time.

## Garmin Connect Has a Hydration Tracker

I noticed that [Garmin Connect](https://connect.garmin.com/) has a built-in hydration feature with proper daily/weekly/monthly views and goal tracking. Much better for spotting patterns than scrolling through calendar events.

I was already using the [Garmin Connect Home Assistant integration](https://github.com/cyberjunky/home-assistant-garmin_connect) to pull fitness data from Garmin into Home Assistant. So the natural question was could I push hydration data back the other way?

## Contributing the Missing Feature

After a bit of exploration, it became clear that the integration didn't support writing hydration data, only reading. So I did what any self-respecting engineer would do, forked the repository, added the `add_hydration` service, and opened a [pull request](https://github.com/cyberjunky/home-assistant-garmin_connect/pull/416).

The change adds an `add_hydration` service action that accepts a value in millilitres and pushes it to Garmin Connect via the [garminconnect Python library](https://github.com/cyberjunky/python-garminconnect).

## The Automation

With the new service in place, I updated my Home Assistant automation. Now a single button press logs to both Google Calendar and Garmin Connect:

```yaml
alias: Log Pint of Water Intake
description: Log 1 pint of water to Google Calendar & Garmin when button 1 is pressed
triggers:
  - entity_id: event.bilresa_dual_button_button_1_6
    trigger: state
conditions:
  - condition: state
    entity_id: event.bilresa_dual_button_button_1_6
    attribute: event_type
    state: multi_press_1
actions:
  - target:
      entity_id: calendar.water_intake
    data:
      summary: Water 1 Pint
      start_date: "{{ now().strftime('%Y-%m-%d') }}"
      end_date: "{{ (now() + timedelta(days=1)).strftime('%Y-%m-%d') }}"
      description: "Logged at: {{ now().strftime('%H:%M:%S') }}"
    action: calendar.create_event
  - target:
      entity_id: sensor.garmin_connect_hydration
    data:
      value_in_ml: 568
    action: garmin_connect.add_hydration
mode: single
```

568 ml is one imperial pint. I have a second automation for button two that logs the exact volume of my tea cups, because if you're going to track hydration, you might as well be precise about it.
