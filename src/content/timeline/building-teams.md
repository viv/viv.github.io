I've led and grown teams at most organisations I've worked for. Hiring well matters, but so does what happens after. I've introduced proper feedback loops, made sure people have space to develop, and invested valuable time in mentoring. One apprentice I mentored went on to win [BCS](https://www.bcs.org/) Software Developer & Tester Apprentice of the Year, and work experience placements became genuine learning experiences for everyone involved.

I've also adapted working practices for colleagues facing mental health challenges. Good teams don't just happen. Someone has to care enough to build them.
