I joined [Hargreaves Lansdown](https://www.hl.co.uk/) as a web developer, building their online life insurance platform, content management system, and trading platform. I was there through the company's flotation on the London Stock Exchange in 2007, when it listed directly onto the FTSE 250.

When the systems you're building underpin a company going through an IPO, there's no room for guesswork. Everything has to work, and keep working. That expectation shaped how I thought about reliability long after I left.
