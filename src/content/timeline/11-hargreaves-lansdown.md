Developer at Hargreaves Lansdown, building the company's online life insurance platform and content management system during the most demanding period in its history, including its flotation on the London Stock Exchange in 2007. They listed directly onto the FTSE 250 and have since been a FTSE 100 company before being taken private.

When systems support a company going through an IPO, "move fast and break things" isn't an option. Reliability under pressure comes from discipline: testing, monitoring, and keeping things simple enough to understand at 2am.
